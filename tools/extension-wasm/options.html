Page photos
Full-size photos
Off
By default the scanner reads a screenshot of the page you scan. If you allow it to read photos on the sites you scan, it can use the full-size picture instead, which is more accurate. Photos are fetched without your cookies and analyzed on this device.
Matching
Look-alike cards
A few cards look like many others to the scanner and win matches they should not. This steers matches away from them. It is off until its accuracy has been confirmed on a separate test set.
