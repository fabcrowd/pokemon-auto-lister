## Product and goal
A personal Chrome MV3 extension (`tools/extension-wasm/`, plain-JS ESM) that identifies and prices Pokemon TCG cards from listing photos on any site (Marketplace, eBay, Mercari, HiBid, Craigslist, Poshmark, Goodwill). The owner's goal: improve **detection, recognition and pricing** from real listing photos, balancing user experience against accuracy. Owner rules, which hold throughout:
- **No price for an unconfirmed card.** Two states only: CONFIRMED or CHOOSE (image-first picking, best match preselected, one tap confirms). Printing (holo/regular/reverse) is the user's pick when inferred only from the picture.
- Stacked or overlapping cards with no clear boundary are out of scope.
- A wrong confirmation must be one-tap correctable and remembered (corrections memory, default on). Unpriceable cards (non-English, merchandise) get a "Can't price this" skip.
- Dollars lost per wrongly confirmed card and taps saved per correct card are the two scores.
- **Never read, list or search `data/sealed-test`.** Never tune to per-photo results. Run `node scripts/sealed-score.mjs` only when the owner asks.
- Never `git add -A`; stage by file name. Commit only when asked; pushes need an allowing permission rule (the permission system blocks public actions otherwise; do not work around it).
- Photos from marketplaces are other people's: keep them under gitignored `.tmp/` or `data/`, never commit. The data repo `fabcrowd/card-scanner-data` is PUBLIC.
